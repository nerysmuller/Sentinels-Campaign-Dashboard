The Protectorate

From the celestial Observatory above to the mortal realms below, The Protectorate spans diverse kingdoms, treacherous dungeons, and ancient ruins. Each location holds secrets of the fallen Celestrians and the path to restoring the divine realm.

The Observatory

Realm of the Almighty - Celestrian Homeland

Once a magnificent fortress floating above the mortal world, the Observatory was the home of the Celestrians and the seat of the Almighty. After a time of divine deliverace foretold, the Celestrian Homeland was struck, in a fury of lightning and darkness. Many Celestrians fell from the sky that day, still nowhere to be found.

The Observatory now lies dormant, its divine power scattered across the mortal realm as benevolessence. Only by gathering enough benevolessence can the Starflight Express be activated to return to the celestial realm and discover what happened to the Almighty.

Current Status: Fallen. The Starflight Express requires benevolessence to function.

Stornway

Kingdom Capital

The largest city in The Protectorate ruled by the monarchs King Schott and Queen Leona. Stornway serves as a major hub for trade and adventure, with its grand castle overlooking the bustling town below. The Quester's Rest tavern and inn, run by Patty and Lyanna, has become a gathering place for adventurers.

Recently, the kingdom was terrorized by the Wight Knight, who'd stolen Princess Anneleise away in the night. After a grueling battle, the Sentinels emerged victorious but not without a cost. Princess Anneleise is still lost to the mortal eye.

Key Locations: Stornway Castle, Quester's Rest Inn, Market Square

Lakewood

Lake Shore

An eerie lake shore north of Stornway, where the Sentinels faced the Wight Knight in battle. After his defeat, many questions were left unanswered, including the whereabouts of Princess Anneleise.

Recent Events: The Wight Knight was defeated here as was the Captain of Stornway's Royal Guard, Geraine, the bastard kid brother of King Schott.

Zere

Heart-Shaped Village

A peaceful village shaped like a heart, surrounded by beautiful zere trees with pink blossoms similar to sakura. A giant zere tree stands proudly in the village center. It's become quite the trade town, where many-a-metalworker has been known to gather materials for smithing.

There is still more to be discovered here.

🌲 Doomingale Forest

Dark Forest

A treacherous forest northwest of Zere, filled with poisonous swamps and dangerous monsters. Deep within this cursed woodland lie the ruins of Brigadoom, the fallen kingdom that the Right Knight once called home.

Dangers: Poisonous purple puddles (cast Safe Passage), strong monsters, difficult navigation

🏔️ Angel Falls

Starting Village

The small village where you awakened after the Observatory fell. Angel Falls is where your mortal journey began, and where you first met Bandit, the odd little critter who can see and talk to Celestrians. Lyanna and the townspeople helped you adjust to life without your wings.

Significance: Your connection point to the mortal world. The place where everything changed.

🚂 The Starflight Express

Divine Train - Currently Inactive

A mystical train capable of traveling between the mortal realm and the Observatory on the celestial plane. Currently, it lacks sufficient benevolessence to function. The Starflight Express reacts to your celestial presence but cannot yet take you back to the realm above.

Current Goal: Gather benevolessence by performing good deeds and helping mortals. Each act of heroism brings you closer to reactivating the train and returning to the Observatory.
