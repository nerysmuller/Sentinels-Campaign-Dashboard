The Protectorate

From the gleaming spires of Lumina to the twisted depths of the Shadowrend Wastes, The Protectorate contains myriad realms of wonder and terror. Each location holds secrets, dangers, and opportunities for those brave enough to explore them.

The Observatory

Sacred Site

At the heart of The Protectorate stands the World Tree, a colossal living monument that once connected the mortal realm to the heavens. Its trunk is so vast that entire villages could be carved into its bark. Its roots delve so deep they touch the Underworld itself, and its branches once reached beyond the clouds to celestial realms.

Now, the World Tree is dying. Its once-golden leaves have turned black and fall like ash. Great cracks split its bark, weeping sap that burns like acid. The area around it has become twisted—reality itself seems unstable near the tree. Demons slip through weak points in the veil, and strange phenomena occur with increasing frequency.

Elder Theron maintains his vigil here, tending the tree with ancient rituals that grow less effective each passing day. The tree's death would mean the complete collapse of the barrier between worlds— unleashing chaos beyond imagination.

Angel Falls

Capital of the Holy Empire

Built around a fragment of fallen celestial architecture, Lumina shines like a beacon across The Protectorate. Its buildings are constructed from white stone that seems to glow with inner light. Towering cathedrals dominate the skyline, their spires reaching toward absent gods.

The city is a study in contrasts—magnificent temples and palaces overlook squalid slums. Inquisitors patrol the streets, hunting heretics and demon-tainted. The Grand Cathedral houses the immortal Emperor-Saint Aurelius, who has ruled for over three centuries.

Key Locations: The Grand Cathedral, The Celestial Archives (largest remaining collection of divine texts), The Radiant Barracks (training ground for paladins), The Inquisitorial Headquarters, The Golden Market

Stornway

Capital of the Free Kingdoms Alliance

A coastal metropolis built into towering cliffs overlooking the Tempest Sea. Stormhaven serves as the meeting place for the Free Kingdoms Alliance, where representatives from dozens of nations gather to debate, scheme, and occasionally cooperate.

The city is known for its massive harbor, where ships from across the world dock to trade goods and information. The Storm Citadel, carved from the cliff itself, houses Queen Cassandra Ashwyn's court and the Alliance Council chambers. The city's lower levels, the Underdocks, are a lawless maze of smugglers, pirates, and worse.

Key Locations: Storm Citadel, The Alliance Forum, Harbor of Ten Thousand Ships, The Underdocks, Stormwall (massive defensive fortification)

The Hexagon

Floating Island of Knowledge

The Academy exists on a massive island that floats through the sky, drifting slowly across The Protectorate. Its position changes seasonally, but its location can always be tracked by the pillar of magical light it projects upward.

Founded centuries ago by a council of archmages, the Academy is politically neutral ground where magic users from all nations can study. Its Great Library contains thousands of tomes on magic theory, history, and practical application. The observatory at the Academy's peak is said to see not just across space, but across time itself.

Key Locations: The Great Library, The Enchantment Forges, The Celestial Observatory, The Proving Grounds (where mages test dangerous spells), Professor Aldrich Vance's Tower

Zere

Cursed Wasteland

Once a thriving kingdom, the Shadowrend Wastes are now a twisted hellscape—the scar left by the Celestial War's climactic battle. The land itself is corrupt, constantly shifting and changing. Buildings phase in and out of reality. Time flows strangely, and travelers may emerge days older or younger than when they entered.

Demons roam freely here, as the barrier between worlds is thinnest in the Wastes. Adventurers come seeking lost treasures from before the war, but few return. Those who do bring back tales of impossible geometries, cities that exist in multiple timeframes simultaneously, and entities that should not exist.

Dangers: Reality distortions, demon incursions, temporal anomalies, the Twisted Citadel (a fortress that appears and disappears), the Field of Fallen Celestrians (where divine bodies still lie)

Coffinwell

Dwarven Undercity

Carved into the roots of the Titan's Spine mountain range, Irondeep is the greatest dwarven city in The Protectorate. Its forges burn eternally, producing weapons and armor of legendary quality. The city extends miles underground, with entire districts dedicated to mining, smithing, and gem-cutting.

Recently, the deepest mines have broken into something ancient and sealed. Strange sounds echo from below. Miners report seeing glowing symbols in languages that predate even the Celestrians. The dwarven king has sealed the Deep Delve, but pressure mounts to investigate what lies beneath.

Key Locations: The Eternal Forge (Garrick Ironforge's workshop), The Hall of Kings, The Crystal Caverns, The Deep Delve (sealed), The Undermarket

Wyrmwood

Headquarters of the Twilight Court

No one knows the true location of the Twilight Spire—it exists partially phased out of reality, accessible only to those who know its secrets. Those who have been there describe it as a tower of black glass that seems to exist in eternal dusk, regardless of the actual time of day.

From here, Mordred Blackthorn coordinates his vast network of spies and assassins. The Spire contains archives rivaling even the Academy's library—knowledge stolen, bought, or killed for. Every secret in The Protectorate eventually makes its way to the Twilight Court, and secrets are the true currency of power.

Alltrades Abbey

Ancient Forest

A primordial forest that predates even the Celestrians. Its trees are impossibly old and vast, their branches forming a canopy so thick that sunlight rarely touches the forest floor. The Elderwood is home to elves, druids, fey creatures, and spirits of nature.

The forest exists partially in the Feywild, creating strange overlaps where reality becomes fluid. Paths shift. Time flows differently. Those who wander off the safe trails may never return, or return centuries later believing only days have passed. The forest is said to be sentient in some way, allowing passage to those it deems worthy and ensnaring those it does not.

Key Locations: The Heartwood Grove (sacred to druids), The Court of Summer (fey nobility), The Whispering Pools (oracle site), The Thornwall (defensive barrier of living plants)

Port Llaffan

City of the Undead

Built upon ancient burial grounds, Necropolis is ruled by Lord Varrick Thornheart and inhabited primarily by vampires, liches, and other intelligent undead. Surprisingly, the city maintains trade relations with living nations, and necromantic magic practiced here is (mostly) legal.

The architecture is Gothic and elaborate, with every building serving as both dwelling and mausoleum. The city never sees sunlight—permanent storm clouds and powerful magics keep it in eternal twilight. Living visitors are permitted but must follow strict protocols. The Great Ossuary beneath the city is said to contain knowledge preserved from civilizations that fell before the Celestial War.

Gleeba

Airship Hub

Floating city dedicated to airship construction and trade. Built on a cluster of magically stabilized islands suspended in the clouds, Aethermoor serves as neutral ground where sky pirates, merchant fleets, and military vessels all dock.

Captain Dorian Stormwind frequents Aethermoor's taverns and black markets. The city is known for its anything-goes atmosphere and spectacular views. Shipwrights here construct vessels capable of traversing not just the sky, but potentially the spaces between planes of reality.

Key Locations: The Cloudtop Tavern, The Aetherdocks, Wrightsmith's Guild, The Storm Cage (prison for dangerous criminals)

Hearthglen

Oracle's Sanctuary

Hidden in the Mist-Shrouded Mountains, this remote temple is where Lyra Moonwhisper receives her visions. The temple was once a Celestrian holy site, and residual divine power allows seers to pierce the veil of time more clearly here.

The journey to reach the temple is treacherous, and the path shifts—only those meant to find it can. Inside, murals depict countless possible futures, updating themselves as prophecies shift and change. The central chamber contains a pool of liquid starlight that shows visions to those who dare look into its depths.

Batsureg

Demon Cult Stronghold

A corrupted holy site in the depths of the Shadowrend Wastes. Once a magnificent temple to the Celestrians, it was twisted during the war into a monument to The Adversary. The Crimson Cult performs their darkest rituals here, sacrificing victims to strengthen the demon lords' influence.

The cathedral exists partially in the Underworld, allowing demons to manifest more easily. Its bells toll constantly, their sound spreading corruption and madness to all who hear. Heroes have attempted to cleanse or destroy the cathedral, but none have succeeded. Some don't return. Others return... changed.

The Sentinel's Rest

Ruined Fortress

Once the primary fortress and training ground of the original Sentinel Order. Now a crumbling ruin scattered with the remains of those who defended it during the Celestial War. Despite its ruined state, the fortress still radiates divine power—demons cannot approach it.

Beneath the ruins lie the Sentinel Crypts, where fallen heroes were laid to rest with their weapons and armor. The Armory of the Ancients supposedly contains legendary artifacts sealed away for safekeeping. Various factions seek to claim the fortress and its treasures, but ancient wards prevent all but those of Sentinel blood from accessing the deepest levels.

Mysterious Locations

These places exist in rumor and legend. Some claim to have found them; others doubt they exist at all...

The Hexagon

A mythical repository said to contain every book that ever was or ever will be written. Vesper the Chronicler supposedly originates from this place. Those who find it gain unlimited knowledge— but at the cost of never being able to leave.

Brigadoom

The seal that binds The Adversary beyond reality. No living being knows its location—the Celestrians hid it well. Prophecy says when the gate is found, the end times begin. The question isn't whether it will be found, but who will find it first.

The Realm of the Almighty

Before they faded, the Celestrians supposedly sealed their greatest weapons and most dangerous artifacts in a vault outside normal space and time. Maps and clues to its location were scattered across The Protectorate, hidden in the ruins of temples and fortresses.
