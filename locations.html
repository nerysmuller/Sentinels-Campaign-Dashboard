The Lands of The Protectorate

From the celestial Observatory above to the mortal realms below, The Protectorate spans diverse kingdoms, treacherous dungeons, and ancient ruins. Each location holds secrets of the fallen Celestrians and the path to restoring the divine realm.

The Observatory

Realm of the Almighty - Celestrian Homeland

Once a magnificent fortress floating above the mortal world, the Observatory was the home of the Celestrians and the seat of the Almighty. When it was destroyed in an attack seemingly unprompted, many fell from the heavens, their whereabouts and status unknown.

The Observatory now lies dormant, its divine power scattered across the mortal realm as benevolessence. Only by gathering enough benevolessence can the Starflight Express be activated to return to the celestial realm and discover what happened to the Almighty.

Current Status: Fallen. The Starflight Express requires benevolessence to function.

The Starflight Express

Divine Train - Currently Inactive

A mystical train capable of traveling between the mortal realm and the Observatory. Currently, it lacks sufficient benevolessence to function. The Starflight Express reacts to your celestial presence but cannot yet take you back to the realm above.

Current Goal: Gather benevolessence by performing good deeds and helping mortals. Each act of heroism brings you closer to reactivating the train and returning to the Observatory.

Zere

Heart-Shaped Village - Current Destination

A peaceful village shaped like a heart, surrounded by beautiful zere trees with pink blossoms similar to sakura. A giant zere tree stands proudly in the village center. It's become quite the trade town, where many-a-metalworker has been known to gather materials for smithing.

There is still more to be discovered here.

Doomingale Forest

Dark Forest

A treacherous forest northwest of Zere, filled with poisonous swamps and dangerous monsters. Deep within this cursed woodland lie the ruins of Brigadoom, the fallen kingdom that the some otherworldly denizens once called home.

Dangers: Poisonous purple puddles (cast Safe Passage), strong monsters, difficult navigation

Stornway

Kingdom Capital - Recently Visited

The largest city in The Protectorate ruled by the monarchs King Schott and Queen Leona. Stornway serves as a major hub for trade and adventure, with its grand castle overlooking the bustling town below. The Quester's Rest tavern and inn, run by Patty and her niece Lyanna, has become a gathering place for adventurers.

Recently, the kingdom was terrorized by the Wight Knight, who'd stolen Princess Anneleise away in the night. After a grueling battle, the Sentinels emerged victorious but not without a cost. Princess Anneleise is still lost to the mortal eye.

Key Locations: Stornway Castle, Quester's Rest Inn, Market Square

The Lakewood

Lake Shore - Battle Site

A serene lakeside location north of Stornway, where the Sentinels faced the Wight Knight in battle. The cursed warrior appeared at nightfall, desperately defending 'his princess' above a pool of mirrored glass. After his defeat, many truths lay unanswered... who was the Wight Knight truly? How did he recognize Althaea's Gitt markings? What are his ties to the Primordial being, Nysoth? And most notably, where is Princess Anneliese if not in his clutches?

Recent Events: The Wight Knight was defeated here, as was Captain Geraine who fought valiantly beside you. He apologized for his actions and asked the party to make amends with King Schott on his behalf before departing to find Brigadoom.

Angel Falls

Starting Village

The small village where you awakened after the Observatory fell. Angel Falls is where your mortal journey began, and where you first met Bandit, the odd little critter who can see and talk to Celestrians. Lyanna and the townspeople helped you adjust to life without your wings.

Significance: Your connection point to the mortal world. The place where everything changed.

Locations Yet to Visit

Many other locations await discovery across The Protectorate. Your journey will take you to diverse kingdoms, ancient ruins, battlefields, and places touched by both divine grace and demonic corruption.

Coffinwell

A town across the Northen Checkpoint with access to a port for sailing eastward. Known for its unique customs and mysteries.

Gleeba

A wealthy merchant city with a dark underbelly. Home to grand bazaars and hidden secrets.

Hearthglen

A small port city suspended off the Slurry Coast. Currently uninhabited due to its destruction half a century ago.

Batsureg

A thriving, Elven-ruled city proper undercover of forested ivy and birch trees. Its hamlets have been known to carry whispers of an entryway to the depths below.

Realm of the Almighty

A divine realm connected to the Observatory. Its current status after the fall is unknown.
