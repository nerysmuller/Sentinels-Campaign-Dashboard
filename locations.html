The Lands of The Protectorate

From the celestial Observatory above to the mortal realms below, The Protectorate spans diverse kingdoms, treacherous dungeons, and ancient ruins. Each location holds secrets of the fallen Celestrians and the path to restoring the divine realm.

The Observatory

Realm of the Almighty - Celestrian Homeland

Once a magnificent fortress floating above the mortal world, the Observatory was the home of the Celestrians and the seat of the Almighty. When the Gittish Empire attempted to destroy it with the Gittingham Express, the Observatory fell from the heavens, and all Celestrians lost their wings and halos.

The Observatory now lies dormant, its divine power scattered across the mortal realm as benevolessence. Only by gathering enough benevolessence can the Starflight Express be activated to return to the celestial realm and discover what happened to the Almighty.

Current Status: Fallen. The Starflight Express requires benevolessence to function.

Stornway

Kingdom Capital - Recently Visited

The largest city in The Protectorate and home to King Schott and Princess Simona. Stornway serves as a major hub for trade and adventure, with its grand castle overlooking the bustling town below. The Quester's Rest inn, run by Erinn and Patty, has become a gathering place for adventurers.

Recently, the kingdom was terrorized by the Wight Knight, who mistook Princess Simona for his long-lost love, Princess Mona of Brigadoom. After the Sentinels resolved this crisis, King Schott opened the royal treasury as a reward, and the town has returned to peace.

Key Locations: Stornway Castle, Quester's Rest Inn, Treasure Chests (accessible after Wight Knight quest), Town Square

🌊 Loch Storn

Lake Shore - Battle Site

A serene lakeside location north of Stornway, where the Sentinels faced the Wight Knight in battle. The cursed warrior appeared at nightfall, searching desperately for his princess. After his defeat, the truth of his identity was revealed—he was the Right Knight of Brigadoom, trapped in undeath by the demon Morag.

Recent Events: The Wight Knight was defeated here. He apologized for his actions and asked the party to make amends with King Schott on his behalf before departing to find Brigadoom.

🌸 Zere

Heart-Shaped Village - Current Destination

A peaceful village shaped like a heart, surrounded by beautiful zere trees with pink blossoms similar to sakura. A giant zere tree stands proudly in the village center. This quiet town is home to Princess Simona's old nanny, Alanna, who knows the nursery rhyme about the Right Knight and Princess Mona.

The village holds the key to finding Brigadoom—Alanna and her friend Petra can sing the ancient song that tells of the Right Knight's quest and the location of the fallen kingdom. The Wight Knight seeks this information desperately.

Important NPCs: Alanna (Princess Simona's former nanny), Petra (Alanna's friend who knows the song)

What You'll Learn: The nursery rhyme "The Right Knight" reveals that he set out to defeat an evil monster but never returned, leaving Princess Mona waiting forever. The song contains clues to finding Brigadoom in Doomingale Forest to the north.

Doomingale Forest

Dark Forest - Contains Brigadoom

A treacherous forest northwest of Zere, filled with poisonous swamps and dangerous monsters. Deep within this cursed woodland lie the ruins of Brigadoom, the fallen kingdom that the Right Knight once called home.

Dangers: Poisonous purple puddles (cast Safe Passage), strong monsters, difficult navigation

💀 Brigadoom (Ruins)

Fallen Kingdom - Dungeon

Once a proud kingdom, Brigadoom now lies in ruins within Doomingale Forest. The Right Knight departed from here centuries ago to defeat the demon Morag, but he never returned. When he finally reaches his homeland, he will discover that Morag awaits him in the throne room—the very monster he failed to defeat.

The ruins are a multi-level dungeon containing treasure chests, dangerous monsters, and the tragic conclusion to the Right Knight's tale. In the throne room sits Morag, the demoness who cursed the knight and caused the kingdom's fall.

Boss: Morag - A powerful demoness who can charm party members with Puff-Puff, drain HP, and use other dark magic. She placed the curse on the Right Knight long ago, and only her defeat can free him.

Treasures: Trailblazing bandana, silk robe, coagulant pouches, clogs, mini medals, springtime skirt, and more scattered throughout the ruins.

🏔️ Angel Falls

Starting Village

The small village where you awakened after the Observatory fell. Angel Falls is where your mortal journey began, and where you first met Stella, the fairy who can see Celestrians. The village elder and the townspeople helped you adjust to life without your wings.

Significance: Your connection point to the mortal world. The place where everything changed.

The Starflight Express

Divine Train - Currently Inactive

A mystical train capable of traveling between the mortal realm and the Observatory. Currently, it lacks sufficient benevolessence to function. The Starflight Express reacts to your celestial presence but cannot yet take you back to the realm above.

Current Goal: Gather benevolessence by performing good deeds and helping mortals. Each act of heroism brings you closer to reactivating the train and returning to the Observatory.

Locations Yet to Visit

Beyond Zere and Brigadoom, many other locations await discovery across The Protectorate. Your journey will take you to diverse kingdoms, ancient ruins, and places touched by both divine grace and demonic corruption.

Coffinwell

A town said to be the next destination after Brigadoom. Known for its unique customs and mysteries.

Gleeba

A wealthy merchant city with a dark underbelly. Home to grand bazaars and hidden secrets.

Swinedimples Academy

A school for young adventurers. An unusual name for an unusual place.

The Gittish Empire

The evil empire responsible for the Observatory's fall. Their Gittingham Palace houses dark secrets and the truth about what happened to the Almighty.

Realm of the Mighty

A divine realm connected to the Observatory. Its current status after the fall is unknown.
