Cartographer's Notes

The Protectorate is a vast world with many secrets yet to discover. As you gather benevolessence and help the people of the realm, new locations will open to you. Some places are hidden, others guarded by powerful enemies, and still others exist in different dimensions accessible only through special means.

The Gittish Empire's attack on the Observatory has changed the world. Divine barriers have weakened, ancient evils stir, and the fate of both the mortal and celestial realms hangs in the balance. Your journey as Sentinels will take you across all corners of The Protectorate—and perhaps beyond.

💫 Special Locations

Grottos: Hidden dungeons that appear across the world. Access them using Treasure Maps dropped by monsters. Each grotto is unique and resets after completion.

Quester's Rest Inn: Has a Tag Mode feature allowing you to connect with other travelers and exchange guests for your inn.

The Starflight Express: Your means of returning to the Observatory. Currently requires more benevolessence to activate.

Alltrades Abbey: Change your vocation here. A crucial location for developing your skills.
