The Underworld - Demon Territories

What is known of the demon realm beneath the world.

⚠️ WARNING: THIS KNOWLEDGE IS FORBIDDEN ⚠️

Limited information exists about the Underworld's structure. What scholars have gleaned comes from interrogating captured cultists and studying ancient texts at great personal risk.

Known Regions:

The Crimson Depths - Domain of Azrael the Doomcaller. Constantly burning lakes of fire and blood. His influence reaches the mortal realm through the Crimson Cult's rituals.

The Frozen Wastes - Paradoxical realm of absolute cold where another Demon Lord dwells. Souls frozen in eternal torment.

The Writhing Palace - Fortress of shifting flesh and bone. Location unknown, purpose unknown, but survivors' accounts speak of horrors beyond description.

Access Points:
• The World Tree's corrupted roots (heavily warded)
• Shadowrend Wastes (unstable tears in reality)
• Crimson Cathedral (ritual site)
• Ancient Seal Locations (if broken)

Cartographer's Notes

Maps of The Protectorate are never truly complete. Reality shifts near the Shadowrend Wastes. The Elderwood's paths change with the seasons. New islands appear in the sky. Demon incursions create temporary rifts to otherworldly realms.

Additionally, many locations are deliberately obscured by magic or lost to time. The Twilight Spire's location is unknown to cartographers. Ancient Sentinel fortresses remain hidden. The Gate of Eternity— if it exists—has left no mark on any map.

As Sentinels, your travels will reveal what has been hidden. Document your discoveries, chart new paths, and perhaps your maps will guide future heroes when darkness rises again.

Legendary Locations - Unconfirmed

Rumors & Myths

The Infinite Library - Said to exist outside normal space. No reliable reports of its location.

The Celestrian Vault - Supposed to contain divine weapons. Treasure hunters seek it constantly. None have returned with proof.

The Gate of Eternity - The seal binding The Adversary. If found, prophecy says the end times begin.

The First Temple - Where the Celestrians first descended to the mortal realm. Lost during the war.

The Mirror Realm - A parallel Protectorate where the Celestrians won the war completely. Supposedly accessible through reflective surfaces under specific conditions.

Note: These locations may or may not exist. Pursue at your own risk.
