The Age of Creation

In the time before memory, when the cosmos was young and the stars yet nameless, the Celestrians—divine beings of immense power—walked among the mortal realms. They shaped mountains with their hands, carved rivers with their breath, and kindled the first flames of civilization in the hearts of mortals.

The greatest of these divine works was The Protectorate, a realm blessed by the heavens themselves. Here, mortal races flourished under celestial guidance: humans learned the arts of war and governance, elves mastered magic and nature's secrets, dwarves delved deep into the earth's mysteries, and countless other races found their place in the grand tapestry of existence.

The Celestrians established the World Tree, Yggdrasil, at the heart of The Protectorate—a living conduit between the mortal realm and the heavens. Its roots stretched into the deepest reaches of the Underworld, while its branches touched the celestial realm itself. Through this sacred tree, divine power flowed into the world, maintaining the delicate balance between light and darkness.

The Celestial War

Peace, however, was not eternal. From the void beyond creation came The Adversary—a primordial force of entropy and malice that sought to unmake all that the Celestrians had built. This being, known by many names across many tongues (the Devourer, the Dark Star, Corruption Incarnate), corrupted several Celestrians to its cause, turning divine protectors into tyrants.

The Celestial War that followed shook the very foundations of reality. Gods fought gods, and the mortal realm became their battlefield. Entire kingdoms were swallowed by darkness. Mountains crumbled. Seas boiled. The sky itself bled.

The Fall

In the war's climactic battle at the World Tree, the loyal Celestrians managed to seal The Adversary beyond the veil of reality—but at terrible cost. The corrupted Celestrians were banished to the Underworld, becoming the first Demon Lords. The World Tree was mortally wounded, its connection to the heavens severed. And the Celestrians themselves, weakened beyond measure, could no longer maintain direct presence in the mortal realm.

As they faded from the world, the Celestrians left behind their final gifts: the Sentinels. These were mortals chosen and blessed with fragments of divine power, charged with protecting The Protectorate from darkness and maintaining the balance in the Celestrians' absence.

The Age of Shadows

A thousand years have passed since the Celestial War. The World Tree still stands, but it is dying—slowly, inexorably. With each passing century, the barriers between worlds grow thinner. Demons slip through the cracks. Dark magic spreads like a plague. Ancient evils, once thought sealed forever, begin to stir.

The original Sentinels are long dead, their order fractured and scattered. Some kingdoms remember the old ways and maintain vigil, while others have descended into corruption and tyranny. The common folk whisper of The Second Adversary—a prophecy that the great evil will return, and when it does, the world will not survive a second war.

The Current State

The Protectorate is a realm divided. Three great powers dominate the political landscape:

  • The Holy Empire of Radiance - A theocratic nation that claims descent from the Celestrians themselves. They seek to reunite The Protectorate under one banner, by force if necessary.
  • The Free Kingdoms Alliance - A confederation of independent realms that resist imperial expansion while struggling with internal conflicts and demonic incursions.
  • The Twilight Court - A mysterious faction that operates in the shadows, claiming to preserve ancient knowledge and maintain the balance between light and dark—but whose true motives remain unclear.

The Call to Arms

Recently, disturbing signs have emerged across The Protectorate:

  • The World Tree's leaves are turning black and falling in unprecedented numbers
  • Demons are appearing with increasing frequency and strength
  • Several Sentinel bloodlines have manifested their dormant powers spontaneously
  • Ancient ruins and temples are activating, displaying cryptic warnings in dead languages
  • A red star has appeared in the sky—the same celestial omen that heralded The Adversary's first coming

It is in this time of gathering darkness that new Sentinels are called—individuals marked by fate, blessed or cursed with power they may not understand. Whether by prophecy, accident, or design, these heroes must rise to face an enemy that destroyed gods... before it destroys everything.

Magic in The Protectorate

Magic flows from the World Tree, permeating reality itself. There are several schools of magical practice:

Celestial Magic

The purest form, drawing directly from divine power. Rare and powerful, practiced primarily by clerics and paladins who maintain faith in the absent Celestrians. Its power has waned as the World Tree weakens.

Arcane Magic

The systematic study of magical formulas and rituals. Wizards and scholars manipulate the fundamental forces of reality through learned techniques. Most common in academic institutions and noble courts.

Primal Magic

Drawing from nature and the raw elements. Druids and rangers tap into the ancient power of the world itself, predating even the Celestrians' arrival.

Shadow Magic

Forbidden arts that draw from the darkness between worlds. Warlocks who bargain with demons and other dark entities wield terrifying power—but at equally terrible cost.

Blood Magic

The most controversial school, using life force itself as a source of power. Outlawed in most nations, yet practiced in secret by those desperate enough to pay its price.

The Sentinel Legacy

Not all who bear power are Sentinels—but all Sentinels bear a mark of destiny. These champions are chosen by forces beyond mortal understanding, marked by one or more of the following signs:

  • A celestial birthmark that glows when divine power is near
  • The ability to perceive threads of fate connecting people and events
  • Dreams or visions of the Celestrial War and events yet to come
  • Unusual resistance to corruption and dark magic
  • The manifestation of powers that exceed normal mortal capabilities

Sentinels rarely understand their purpose immediately. Some spend years searching for meaning, while others have destiny thrust upon them by circumstance. What unites them all is an inexorable pull toward the conflicts that will determine The Protectorate's fate.

The old Sentinel Order maintained temples, archives, and training facilities across the realm. Most have fallen to ruin, been claimed by other factions, or simply lost to time. Recovering these sacred sites and the knowledge they contain may be crucial to stopping the darkness that rises.

Prophecies and Omens

Ancient texts speak of signs that herald the return of The Adversary. Many of these have come to pass:

  • ✓ "When the leaves of the World Tree turn to ash..."
  • ✓ "When the red star bleeds upon the northern sky..."
  • ✓ "When those of divine blood awaken to their power..."
  • ⚠ "When the three kingdoms stand divided..."
  • ? "When the last seal is broken and the gate stands open..."
  • ? "When the Chosen Seven gather at the dying tree..."

The final lines remain unclear. Some scholars believe "the last seal" refers to ancient prisons holding the Demon Lords. Others think it speaks of the barrier between worlds entirely. As for the "Chosen Seven"— this could refer to seven specific Sentinels, or perhaps something else entirely.

Time will tell. But time, it seems, is running short.
