🌌 THE CHRONICLE OF ALTHELAS

The World Before the Fall

(As preserved in the Celestrian Archives of the Observatory)

🌙 THE TIME BEFORE TIME

Before the first dawn, there were two presences—Zenus, the Architect, and his daughter Celestria, the Light of Mercy.

From where they came, no one knows. Some say Zenus emerged from the breath of the cosmos itself; others whisper he is the echo of Irkala's dying dream. Together they shaped the heavens and laid the foundations for what would become the world of Althelas.

From Zenus's design came Yggdrasil, the World Tree—a living bridge between heaven and earth, the source of all light and creation. But creation invited hunger, and from the shadows beneath Irkala's roots rose those who would unmake it.

⚙️ ERA I — THE AGE OF CHAINS

(The First Sundering)

  • • The world thrived under Irkala, the Primordial Earth, who sang the roots of life into being.
  • • Her son, Therig, born of ambition and shadow, slew her and seized her power.
  • • With the aid of Thal'kyr, the Whispering Shadow, and Zarathul, the Abyssal Flame, Therig hacked Yggdrasil apart, scattering her limbs and sealing her heart in the deep.
  • • In her death cry, Irkala's lifeblood birthed the Twelve Disciples, divine fragments of her virtues, who became Therig's slaves.

This act fractured the heavens from the earth—the First Sundering—and the world's first age ended in tyranny and silence.

🔆 ERA II — THE AGE OF TWINS

From Therig's fractured mind were born the goddesses Imera and Nychta, daughters of day and night, born in balance. Together they overthrew their father and cast him into the Abyss, where Thal'kyr and Zarathul followed him in ruin.

For a time, peace returned. But jealousy and love warred in Nychta's heart. Her duel with Imera—the Monomachia sto Thanato—split the firmament again. Imera's blood rained upon the dead roots of Yggdrasil, awakening her first small bloom.

The heavens dimmed. The world entered twilight. This was not yet the Second Sundering, but it paved the road to it.

🔮 ERA III — THE AGE OF FORGOTTEN GODS

Beneath creation, the Forgotten Ones stirred—Primordials who predated the gods themselves. Their domains governed all extremes: creation, despair, chaos, storm, and judgment.

These beings became legend—their influence now seen only in storm, hunger, and dream.

⚔️ ERA IV — THE AGE OF THE FIVE

(The Second Sundering)

When mortals grew arrogant and forgot the divine, the world shook again. Therig returned from the Abyss, wearing the devoured powers of Thal'kyr and Zarathul—now the Ashen Architect, god of destruction reborn.

Five heroes rose to oppose him—the Five: Astathis, Toshiko, Croak Box, Fennen, and Althaea. They fought in the War of the Feybranche, named for the living branch cut from Yggdrasil's heart.

Their victories were earned in blood, and their triumph required sacrifice:

  • Astathis froze himself within Yggdrasil's roots, channeling his soul to restore her.
  • Vorathos gave his essence to seal the Abyss.
  • The remaining Four gave up their divinity to mend the world's rift.
  • The twin sisters relinquished immortality, believing mortals must rule their own fate.

The world trembled. Continents sank. Skies tore. This was the Second Sundering, and from its ruin bloomed the new Yggdrasil—radiant and whole for the first time since Irkala's death.

🌿 ERA V — THE AGE OF CELESTRIANS

From the blossoms of Yggdrasil came beings of light—the Celestrians, guardians of gratitude and mortal hope. Zenus and his daughter Celestria appointed them to gather Benevolessence, the purified essence of mortal kindness, to feed Yggdrasil's renewal.

Their shining home, the Observatory, floated among the stars. There they awaited the day Yggdrasil would bear Fyggs, golden fruit that would open the Starlight Express, the celestial train that would carry them to paradise.

Centuries passed. Benevolessence waned. Mortals prayed less, fought more. The roots of Yggdrasil grew thin.

☄️ ERA VI — THE FALL OF THE HEAVENS

When the Fyggs at last began to glow, a storm of impossible light tore the heavens apart. The Starlight Express was struck down, crashing through the clouds. The Observatory followed—burning as it fell.

Celestrians plunged to the world below, their wings torn away, halos silenced. The flow of Benevolessence inverted—the world's gratitude turned to grief, and from that grief came the Blight.

This is where your story begins.

✴️ ERA VII — THE AGE OF THE SENTINELS

(The Present Age)

Now, the Protectorate of Althelas endures under mortal kings and fractured faiths. Yggdrasil withers. The Blight spreads through the soil and soul alike.

A group of fallen Celestrians—the Sentinels—wander the land, gathering Benevolessence anew, hoping to rekindle the light of heaven. Their mentor Aquila vanished in the fall, though his voice echoes faintly from the ghost of the Starflight Express.

Meanwhile, whispers rise from the north of a Winged One stirring in the shadows, rallying the Gitt Empire in search of "the true gods"—Primordials long imprisoned in the Abyss.

And as mortals cling to dying faith, the heavens wait—silent, but listening.

⭐ Your Place in History ⭐

You are Sentinels, fallen Celestrians who walk the mortal world gathering Benevolessence—the essence of gratitude and hope. Your quest is to rekindle the light of Yggdrasil, repair the Starlight Express, and return to the Observatory to discover what happened to the Almighty.

But darkness stirs. The Blight spreads. Ancient powers awaken. And somewhere in the north, a Winged One gathers armies in the name of forgotten gods.

The fate of Althelas rests in your hands.
