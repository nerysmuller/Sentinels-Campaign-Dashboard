The Denizens of The Protectorate

From Celestrian guardians to mortal monarchs, from tragic knights to demonic threats, The Protectorate is home to countless souls whose fates intertwine with yours. Learn their stories, remember their names, and choose your allies wisely.

Your Companions

Bandit

Unlikely Animal Guide

A loyal raccoon who can seemingly see and communicate with Celestrians even after they lost their wings. Bandit has traveled with you since Angel Falls, offering guidance, encouragement, and companionship on your quest to gather benevolessence and return to the Observatory. But even she seems to know too much, and it's obvious there's more to her than meets the eye.

Ally

Aquila

Former Mentor & Knight Protector of the Celestrian Order

Your teacher in the Observatory before the fall. Aquila's current whereabouts and condition after losing his wings remain a mystery. He taught you much about being a Celestrian and protecting the mortal realm.

Unknown

The Almighty

God of the Observatory

The supreme being who ruled the Observatory and commanded the Celestrians. When the Gittish Empire attacked, the Almighty's fate became uncertain. What happened to them during the fall? This mystery drives your quest.

Unknown

People of Stornway

King Schott

King of Stornway

A concerned ruler who genuinely cares for his kingdom and daughter. He cares deeply about Stornoway and its people, What is currently focused on locating his daughter as well as dealing with the loss of his bastard kid brother and former Captain of the Stornyway Guard, Geraine.

Strained

Queen Leona

Queen of Stornway

The fierce steadfast, female monarch of Stornway Proper. Both she and her husband rule with an iron fist, all the while advocating for their people and the diversity of the cities denizens.

Strained

Geraine Montigue

Former Captain of the Stornway Guard

A brilliant fighter with a troubled past, the former captain of the Stornway Guard joined your party briefly to take on the Wight Knight at the Lakewood. During your preparations for the battle, you found out he was not only brilliant in terms of fighting prowess, but also intelligence, having picked up and known a lot more about your party than any normal fellow should.

Deceased

Princess Anneliese

Princess of Stornway

The kind-hearted princess whom the Wight Knight stole away in the night. After seeing her reflection in the Lakewood mirror pool, you heard her voice, but we're never able to locate her. She is still nowhere to be found.

Unknown

Lyanna

Innkeeper at Quester's Rest

The cheerful proprietor of Quester's Rest, Stornway's premier inn and tavern for adventurers. Lyanna provides lodging, banking, and even alchemical services. Her inn has become a home base for your adventures.

Ally

Patty

Innkeeper at Quester's Rest

Lyanna's witty and not-at-all-demure aunt. You saved her from the beholder in the depths of the Hexagon, not too long ago. The Quester's Rest is her pride and joy, and she seems to run the place with just a touch of magic. Known for her fantastic homecooking and rather strong, eclectic brews, she's made this inn and tavern "the place to be" in Stornway Proper.

Ally

The Wight Knight

Cursed Warrior

Once a handsome and noble knight of Brigadoom, he was the beloved of Princess Mona. When he set out to defeat the demon Morag, he was cursed and trapped in an undead state for centuries. Recently awakened by the earthquake from the Gittish Empire's attack, he mistook Princess Simona for his lost love and terrorized Stornway. After you defeated him, he realized his error and now seeks to find Brigadoom and finally complete his quest to defeat Morag.

"The Right Knight"

The town is full of laughter,

An upbeat, but tragic tune some of your party heard on your travels north to the Lakewood. It was an old nursery rhyme as you recall.

Other Celestrians

Before the Observatory fell, many Celestrians watched over the mortal realm. Their fates after losing their wings and halos remain largely unknown. Some may have perished, others may walk among mortals unaware of their true nature, and still others might be searching for answers just as you are.
