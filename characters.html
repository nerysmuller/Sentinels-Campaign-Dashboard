The Denizens of The Protectorate

From Celestrian guardians to mortal monarchs, from tragic knights to demonic threats, The Protectorate is home to countless souls whose fates intertwine with yours. Learn their stories, remember their names, and choose your allies wisely.

Your Companions

Stella

Celestrian Fairy - Your Guide

A loyal fairy who can see and communicate with Celestrians even after they lost their wings. Stella has traveled with you since Angel Falls, offering guidance, encouragement, and companionship on your quest to gather benevolessence and return to the Observatory.

Ally

Aquila

Former Celestrian Mentor

Your teacher in the Observatory before the fall. Aquila's current whereabouts and condition after losing his wings remain a mystery. He taught you much about being a Celestrian and protecting the mortal realm.

Unknown

The Almighty

God of the Observatory

The supreme being who ruled the Observatory and commanded the Celestrians. When the Gittish Empire attacked, the Almighty's fate became uncertain. What happened to them during the fall? This mystery drives your quest.

Unknown

People of Stornway

King Schott

King of Stornway

A concerned ruler who genuinely cares for his kingdom and daughter. When the Wight Knight threatened Princess Simona, King Schott sought heroes to protect his people. After you defeated the knight and resolved the crisis peacefully, he gratefully opened the royal treasury as thanks.

Ally

Princess Simona

Princess of Stornway

The kind-hearted princess whom the Wight Knight mistook for Princess Mona. After learning the truth about the tragic knight's story, Simona asked you to visit her old nanny Alanna in Zere to learn more about Brigadoom and help the Wight Knight find peace.

Ally

Erinn

Innkeeper at Quester's Rest

The cheerful proprietor of Quester's Rest, Stornway's premier inn for adventurers. Erinn provides lodging, banking services, and discovered the Krak Pot for alchemy. Her inn has become a home base for your adventures.

Ally

Patty

Party Organizer

Runs Patty's Party Planning Place at Quester's Rest. She helped you form your adventuring party and can create new companions or modify existing ones. Patty's services are invaluable for building a balanced team of heroes.

Ally

The Tragic Tale of Brigadoom

The Wight Knight / The Right Knight

Cursed Warrior

Once a handsome and noble knight of Brigadoom, he was the beloved of Princess Mona. When he set out to defeat the demon Morag, he was cursed and trapped in an undead state for centuries. Recently awakened by the earthquake from the Gittish Empire's attack, he mistook Princess Simona for his lost love and terrorized Stornway. After you defeated him, he realized his error and now seeks to find Brigadoom and finally complete his quest to defeat Morag.

Ally

Princess Mona

Lost Love - Princess of Brigadoom

The Right Knight's betrothed, who waited for him to return from his quest to defeat Morag. When he never came back, she waited in Brigadoom... but what became of her when the kingdom fell remains to be seen. The nursery rhyme speaks of their tragedy: a knight who never returns and a princess doomed to wait for eternity.

Unknown

Morag

Demoness of Brigadoom

The demon who cursed the Right Knight and caused Brigadoom's fall. She sits waiting in the throne room of the ruined kingdom, a powerful sorceress capable of charm, life-draining attacks, and dark curses. When the Right Knight finally reaches the throne room, Morag will curse him again—this time forcing him to kneel helplessly until she is defeated.

Enemy

People of Zere

Alanna

Princess Simona's Former Nanny

An elderly woman living in Zere who once served as Princess Simona's nursemaid. Alanna knows the nursery rhyme "The Right Knight," which tells of Brigadoom's tragedy and contains clues to the kingdom's location. She can sing the song with her friend Petra—and this knowledge is exactly what the Wight Knight desperately seeks.

Ally

Petra

Alanna's Friend

A resident of Zere and close friend of Alanna. Together, they can perform the complete nursery rhyme about the Right Knight and Princess Mona, revealing the location of Brigadoom in the Doomingale Forest to the north.

Ally

The Gittish Empire

The Gittish Empire

Dark Power

The malevolent force responsible for the Observatory's fall. Using the Gittingham Express, they attempted to destroy the Realm of the Almighty, causing the catastrophic earthquake that awakened the Wight Knight and changed your fate forever. Their true goals and the extent of their power remain mysterious.

Enemy

Emperor Godwyn

Gittish Emperor

The ruler of the Gittish Empire. Little is known about him at this stage of your journey, but his empire's actions brought down the Observatory. What drives him? What is his connection to the Almighty? These questions await answers.

Enemy

The Nursery Rhyme

"The Right Knight"

The Right Knight sets off on his steed,
In search of evil he rides.
If he can defeat the terrible beast,
He'll be home to wed his bride.

The town is full of laughter,
Preparing a feast fit for a queen,
Och! But then disaster!
The Right Knight's nowhere to be seen!

Bird, north, Brigadoomward on,
Tell her that her knight is gone,
Bird, north, Brigadoomward on,
Tell her that her knight is goooone!

This tragic song holds the clues to finding Brigadoom—north from Zere, in the Doomingale Forest, where the Right Knight's story reaches its conclusion.

Other Celestrians

Before the Observatory fell, many Celestrians watched over the mortal realm. Their fates after losing their wings and halos remain largely unknown. Some may have perished, others may walk among mortals unaware of their true nature, and still others might be searching for answers just as you are.

Pavo

Celestrian

A Celestrian who can be found at Quester's Rest. Pavo teaches about the Rapportal system that allows Celestrians to cooperate across realms.

Ally

Other Fallen Celestrians

Unknown Number

Countless other Celestrians fell when the Observatory was destroyed. Their current whereabouts and conditions are unknown. You may encounter them on your journey.
