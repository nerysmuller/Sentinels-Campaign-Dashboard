The Denizens of The Protectorate

From divine emissaries to demonic warlords, from noble knights to cunning rogues, The Protectorate is home to countless beings of power and influence. Some may become allies in your quest; others will stop at nothing to see you fail. Learn their faces, remember their names, and choose your alliances wisely.

Major NPCs

Seraphina Luminis

High Inquisitor of the Empire

A zealous paladin who leads the Holy Empire's military forces. She genuinely believes in reuniting The Protectorate under divine law, but her methods are brutal and uncompromising.

Neutral

Mordred Blackthorn

The Raven King

Enigmatic leader of the Twilight Court. Appears as a charming nobleman but commands vast networks of spies and assassins. His true goals remain shrouded in mystery.

Unknown

Elder Theron

Keeper of the World Tree

An ancient elf druid who has tended the World Tree for centuries. One of the few who remembers the Celestial War firsthand. Weak and fading as the tree dies.

Ally

Vesper

The Chronicler

A mysterious entity that appears as a hooded figure surrounded by floating tomes. Claims to record all events in The Protectorate's history. Speaks in riddles and prophecies.

Neutral

Lady Cassandra Ashwyn

Queen of the Northern Kingdoms

A brilliant tactician and diplomat who leads the Free Kingdoms Alliance. Haunted by visions of her kingdom's destruction. Seeks to unite the alliance against all threats.

Ally

Azrael the Doomcaller

Demon Lord

One of the corrupted Celestrians imprisoned in the Underworld. His influence spreads through dark cults. Whispers promises of power to those desperate or foolish enough to listen.

Enemy

Garrick Ironforge

Master Artificer

Dwarven genius who crafts magical weapons and armor. Gruff exterior hides a warm heart. One of the few who can still forge Celestial steel using ancient techniques.

Ally

The Crimson Wraith

Assassin

A legendary killer whose true identity is unknown. Serves the highest bidder. Some claim they are a ghost, others a demon, still others a former Sentinel gone rogue.

Enemy

Professor Aldrich Vance

Archmage Scholar

Leading expert on Celestrian history and magic at the Academy of the Arcane. Eccentric and absent-minded but possesses encyclopedic knowledge of ancient lore.

Ally

Lyra Moonwhisper

Oracle of Shadows

A young seer cursed with visions of possible futures. Dwells in a remote temple, seeking Sentinels to help prevent the darkest timelines she foresees.

Ally

Roguish airship captain with a heart of gold (and pockets full of stolen goods). Commands the fastest vessel in the skies. Loyal to coin, but more loyal to friends.

Ally

Practitioner of forbidden blood magic. Claims to fight darkness with darkness. The Empire has a substantial bounty on her head. Her methods are effective but disturbing.

Factions & Organizations

The Holy Empire of Radiance

Theocratic superpower seeking to unite The Protectorate under celestial law. Powerful military, zealous inquisitors, and vast resources. Led by the immortal Emperor-Saint Aurelius, who claims direct divine mandate.

The Free Kingdoms Alliance

Coalition of independent nations resisting imperial expansion. Diverse cultures and approaches to governance. Strength in unity, but plagued by internal disagreements and competing interests.

The Twilight Court

Shadow organization operating across all nations. Master spies, assassins, and information brokers. True goals unknown. Some believe they protect ancient secrets; others think they serve dark powers.

The Sentinel Order (Remnants)

Scattered descendants of the original divine champions. No central leadership remains. Small cells operate independently, preserving what knowledge and techniques they can. Seeking to rebuild.

The Academy of the Arcane

Premier magical institution. Politically neutral in theory, though individual factions within vie for influence. Houses the largest library of magical knowledge outside the destroyed Celestrian temples.

The Crimson Cult

Demon-worshipping fanatics seeking to hasten The Adversary's return. Cells hidden throughout society. Practice forbidden rituals and sacrifice. More organized and widespread than authorities acknowledge.

Legendary Figures

These beings exist in history, myth, or something in between. Whether they will appear in your journey remains to be seen...

Celestria the Lightbringer

First Celestrian

Mother of the Celestrians, who shaped The Protectorate from primordial chaos. Legends say she didn't fade with the others but transformed into the World Tree itself, giving her essence to sustain the realm. If this is true, the tree's death means...

The First Sentinel

Name Lost to Time

The original mortal champion blessed by the Celestrians. Their name has been erased from history, but legends speak of impossible feats: slaying a Demon Lord single-handedly, holding back the darkness with nothing but will, and making a sacrifice so great the gods themselves wept.

The Adversary

The Unnamed Evil

The primordial force of destruction sealed beyond reality. Its true nature is unknowable—some believe it to be a dark god, others an elemental force, still others the embodiment of cosmic entropy itself. Prophecy says it will return. The only question is when.
