Recent Developments

Session 1

Called from the quiet of eternity, you were reborn as Celestrians beneath Aquila’s watch, only to witness the Observatory’s ruin and tumble to the mortal world stripped of grace.

Session 2

In the small village of Angel Falls, mortal gratitude and sorrow intertwined, and the first motes of Benevolessence found their way back to you through human hands.

Session 3

The ruins below the cliffs whispered of divine experiments and forgotten love; there, you confronted the Blight’s first breath and the echo of a fallen Celestrian.

Session 4

Upon your travels to Stornway, you uncovered the turmoil of the monarchy as a man calling himself "The Wight Knight" terrorized the city proper. The heavens stirred as visions of dragons and divine war clawed at the edges of memory.

Session 5

Under a ghostly moon at the Northern Lakewood, the Wight Knight’s sorrow met its end... and Geraine’s life ended with it, his final act saving the realm he swore to guard.

Session 6

Carrying Geraine’s body home, you mourned a friend, glimpsed Aquila’s light across the veil, and felt the call to something darker... deeper, as you head northeast to the village of Zere.
